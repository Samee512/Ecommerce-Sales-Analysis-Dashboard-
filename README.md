# Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard is a comprehensive Power BI project designed to analyze online sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, profitability trends, and category-wise business performance. The dashboard transforms raw ecommerce data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and KPI-driven analytics.

This project focuses on tracking sales amount, profit generation, order quantity, payment preferences, customer contribution, and product category performance. The dashboard enables business stakeholders and analysts to make informed decisions using data-driven insights.

---

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this dashboard are:

- Monitor overall ecommerce sales performance
- Analyze profit trends across different months
- Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories
- Track customer purchasing contribution
- Analyze state-wise sales distribution
- Understand customer payment preferences
- Support business decision-making through interactive analytics

---

## Technology Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following technologies and tools:

### Power BI Desktop
Used as the primary data visualization and dashboard development platform.

### Power Query
Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing to ensure data consistency and quality.

### DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
Implemented for creating calculated measures, KPIs, profit analysis, and dynamic visual calculations.

### Data Modeling
Relationships were established among multiple tables to enable efficient filtering, aggregation, and cross-analysis.

### File Formats
- `.pbix` for Power BI project files
- `.png` for dashboard previews and documentation

## Data Source

The dataset used in this project contains ecommerce sales transaction data, including:

- Customer names
- Product categories and sub-categories
- Sales amount
- Quantity ordered
- Profit values
- Payment modes
- State-wise sales data
- Monthly sales and profit trends

The data was structured to support multidimensional sales and profitability analysis.

## State-wise Sales Analysis

A horizontal bar chart visualizes sales distribution across different states.

### Key Insights
- Maharashtra generates the highest sales revenue
- Madhya Pradesh contributes significantly to overall sales
- Uttar Pradesh and Delhi show moderate sales performance

This analysis helps identify high-performing regions for business expansion and marketing strategies.

---

## Quantity by Category Analysis

A donut chart represents product quantity distribution across major categories:

- Clothing
- Electronics
- Furniture

### Key Insights
- Clothing contributes the highest share of total quantity sold
- Electronics maintain a strong sales contribution
- Furniture represents a smaller but valuable segment

This analysis supports inventory planning and category-level business optimization.

---

## Monthly Profit Analysis

A column chart tracks profit trends across all months.

### Key Insights
- Highest profits were generated in January and December
- Some months experienced negative profit margins
- Seasonal trends can be identified for better sales planning

This visualization helps businesses understand profitability patterns throughout the year.

---

## Customer-wise Sales Analysis

A horizontal bar chart displays sales contribution by top customers.

### Key Insights
- Harivansh generated the highest sales contribution
- Madhav and Madan Mohan are among the top customers
- Customer-level insights support loyalty and retention strategies

This analysis helps businesses identify valuable customers and target them effectively.

---

## Payment Mode Analysis

A donut chart visualizes quantity distribution by payment methods:

- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- UPI
- Debit Card
- Credit Card
- EMI

### Key Insights
- COD is the most preferred payment method
- UPI has strong adoption among customers
- Digital payment methods contribute significantly to transactions

This analysis helps businesses optimize payment options and customer convenience.

---

## Profit by Sub-Category Analysis

A horizontal bar chart compares profit contribution across product sub-categories:

- Printers
- Bookcases
- Sarees
- Accessories
- Tables

### Key Insights
- Printers generate the highest profit
- Bookcases and Sarees contribute strong profitability
- Tables generate comparatively lower profits

This analysis helps identify profitable product segments for business growth.
